# How Is the Shop Performing? — Case Study

Analysis of an online shop's performance from Jan 2024 to Jun 2026, using
customers, orders, products and payments data.

## Files
- `shop_performance_pandas_notebook.py` — Databricks notebook: cleaning, joins, analysis
- `shop_clean.csv` — cleaned dataset (49,830 rows)
- `shop_dashboard.xlsx` — pivot tables, charts and dashboard
- `shop_performance_presentation.pptx` — presentation with findings and recommendations

## Key findings
- Total revenue: 2,988,380 from 42,696 completed and paid orders
- Revenue dropped about 32% from February 2026
- Electronics is the top category (51% of revenue)
- Deep discounts (20–30%) lower order value without increasing units sold

## Recommendations
1. Investigate the February 2026 revenue drop
2. Cap discounts at 10%
3. Promote high-revenue products and strengthen the VIP tier
